# Resume Standards

## Formatting

- Exactly one page. Trim or rewrite lower-value material before reducing type below 10.5 pt.
- Use Times New Roman. Body text is at least 10.5 pt; section headers are about 12 pt bold; the name is about 15-16 pt bold.
- Use margins near top 0.3-0.5 in, bottom 0.2-0.3 in, and left/right 0.4 in. Default to a 0.5 in top margin when space allows.
- Center bold section headers and place a consistent divider beneath each. Do not add a meta-title such as "Tailored Resume."
- Set bullets to true single spacing with about a 0.5 in left indent and a 0.25 in hanging indent. Wrapped lines must align cleanly.
- Use about 3-5 pt before a new role or project and 7-8 pt before major sections. Preserve visible breathing room rather than crushing content.
- Align dates consistently on the right.
- Bold meaningful, supported quantitative evidence only; do not bold generic skills or product jargon.
- Spell out each degree's official full name in the Education section; do not use degree abbreviations. Use only the degree information supplied by the candidate. If the full name is absent or an abbreviation is ambiguous, ask a focused question before finalizing rather than inferring it.

## Bullet quality

Write action-led bullets as complete, skimmable stories. A reader should be able to tell what the candidate did, the business or user need it served, and the outcome without piecing together distant clauses. Put the action and its purpose close together; then make the result concrete. Do not write a feature, audience, or tool name as a substitute for the reason the work mattered.

Prioritize impact, using legitimate metrics when available. Use strong but accurate action verbs, and show communication, influence, collaboration, negotiation, customer empathy, and decision-making through the work itself rather than listing soft skills in the Skills section. Make scope, stakeholders, tradeoffs, validation, and implementation details earn their place by clarifying why the candidate is a strong fit.

Use job-description keywords deliberately. When a required keyword is central to the supported story, it may lead the bullet if that produces a natural, compelling opening. Do not force every bullet to begin with a keyword or turn the resume into a keyword list; human readability and truthful storytelling are equally required.

Before finalizing, check that the bullets collectively show relevant needs, actions, judgment, collaboration, validation, and outcomes rather than a task inventory. Quantify legitimate impact. Use ownership language precisely:

- **Owned** for direct responsibility or decision authority.
- **Led** for driving or coordinating work.
- **Built** for implementation.
- **Shaped** for material influence on direction.
- **Contributed** for participation without ownership.

## Google Docs QA loop

Use a local rendered preview for the user's content-and-layout review before publishing. Google Docs is the final visual source of truth after the user approves. Inspect the actual Google Doc after creation and repeat editing and inspection until all checks pass:

1. The document is exactly one page.
2. Body text is at least 10.5 pt and bullets are true single-spaced.
3. Dates, indentation, wrapped bullets, role spacing, and section headers are aligned and visually consistent.
4. Measure the gap from the final line of content to the bottom margin. More than 0.5 in of unused space is excessive and requires another content-selection pass. If it is long, remove or shorten lower-value content in this order: generic supporting detail, lower-relevance material, then less meaningful accomplishments.
5. If it is sparse, revisit both the source resume and the job-description gap analysis. Add only supported material that improves the reader's understanding of fit: a stronger explanation of the project or problem, a key decision, relevant implementation detail, customer or stakeholder context, validation or evaluation, a measurable result, or evidence that addresses a weak job requirement. Select the best story for the role rather than mechanically adding a particular evidence category. Do not add filler.
6. The final standalone Google Doc is in the requested Drive location with the requested naming convention.

## Pre-publish review

Render the staging document at a legible scale and present the page preview in the conversation before any Drive upload. The preview must make the page count, margins, bottom whitespace, and wrapped bullets readily visible. Invite feedback and wait for the user's approval before publishing. If the user asks for revisions, update the staging document and show a new preview. The user may explicitly waive this checkpoint for a particular request.
